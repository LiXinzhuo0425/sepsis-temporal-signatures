# Version 2.1.0 (2026-09-06)

This release aligns the public code and derived data with *Early longitudinal trajectories and rank persistence of published blood RNA signatures in sepsis: a multicohort transcriptomic study*.

- Updated the analysis-ready and figure-source workbooks from the final study files, including the GSE106878 treatment-arm sensitivity and its distinction from the frozen descriptive strata.
- Added the final cohort-selection figure, six exact main-figure TIFF files, and the supplementary appendix with Tables S1–S13 and Figures S1–S6.
- Clarified 302 unique case IDs, 299 patients with an observed baseline, 172 T24 pairs, 146 T48 pairs, and 264 unique patients contributing an early pair. These descriptive counts do not change the frozen paired values or primary synthesis.
- Updated portable code and paths for the treatment sensitivity, selection figure, and supplementary Figure S6 source tables.
- Normalized venue wording and filenames, retained scientific provisions and dates in the analysis plan, and rebuilt integrity manifests.

The eight published formulas, six-cohort primary analysis, and separate 47-patient GSE106878 replication remain unchanged. Treatment-arm sensitivity is not an efficacy analysis. Earlier figures and reproduction records are explicitly retained as historical materials. The final supplied TIFFs take precedence over historical alternative exports.
